# Data Analyst II, Acme Corp (Austin, TX)

Requisition REQ-4417.

Acme Corp builds heavy drilling equipment for mining and infrastructure customers. Our operations analytics team turns sensor, service and warranty data into decisions that keep machines running in the field. We are adding a Data Analyst II to support the team's growing reporting load.

## Responsibilities

- Support the ZX-9000 service team with weekly warranty reports.
- Maintain the dashboards that operations leads use to track parts inventory and repair turnaround.
- Answer ad hoc questions from the service and supply chain teams, and document recurring ones so they can be automated.

## Requirements

- 2+ years in an analytics role, with strong SQL.
- Familiarity with dbt and a willingness to learn our modelling conventions.

## Compensation and visa

Base salary range $92,000 to $108,000. Acme does not sponsor visas for this role. On-site five days a week in the Austin office. Benefits include medical, dental and vision coverage, a 401(k) match and an annual training budget.

Hiring manager: Priya Natarajan. The team reports to Luis Ortega, VP of Operations.
