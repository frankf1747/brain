# Settling in Austin

Date: 2026-03-02

I live in Austin now, in a one-bedroom apartment in East Austin. My lease runs for twelve months, through February 2027. The place is small but bright, with a window over the kitchen sink that gets the morning sun, and the landlord fixed the leaking shower head within a week of my asking, which already puts this apartment ahead of the last one. The laundry room is shared and the dryer eats coins, so I keep a jar of quarters by the door.

The commute is the part I am still getting used to. The bus to the office takes about forty minutes when it runs on time, so I have started cycling on dry days; it is twenty-five minutes and I arrive more awake than I do on the bus. Groceries are a short walk away, and there is a taco truck on the corner that I have already visited more often than I would admit to anyone.

Paperwork: I am on F-1 OPT, so I put my I-20, the EAD card, my passport and the offer letter in one blue folder in the desk drawer, with scans in a cloud folder as well. If anyone ever asks for them, I want to find them in a minute, not an afternoon.

Plans for the spring. At work, finish the dbt migration I started in January and write down how the models are reviewed, so the next person does not have to guess. Outside work, update my resume and start applying to analytics roles in Texas, mostly in Austin and Dallas, by April. I also want to find a weekend running group, and learn to cook at least three dinners that are not pasta.
