# Job search priorities, end of September

Date: 2026-09-28

Writing these down so I stop re-deciding them every time a recruiter calls.

1. Roles. I am targeting analytics lead or senior data analyst roles. Junior titles are out unless the scope is clearly senior, and I am not looking at pure data engineering jobs right now.
2. Money. My minimum base salary is $130,000. Equity is nice but I will not count it toward that number.
3. Ranking. Northwind Robotics is my first choice and Acme Corp is my second.
4. Location. Any role must be based in Denver or fully remote, because I moved to Denver this month. I am not signing up for a weekly flight.
5. Visa. I still need an employer that will sponsor an H-1B. I should raise it in the first call, not the last round.

Why Northwind first: the analytics lead post is exactly the job I want, the office is here, and Wei Zhang, who now works at Northwind, says the analytics team is growing. Wei and I worked together for two years, and he does not oversell things.

Why Acme second: I like Priya and the case study was fun, but the role wants three days a week in Austin. Relocation help does not change the fact that I just moved.

Things I will not do again:
- Spend a weekend on a take-home assignment before anyone has told me the salary range.
- Let a process drift for a month without asking for a decision date.
