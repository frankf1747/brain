# Analytics Lead, Northwind Robotics (Denver, CO)

Requisition NWR-0912.

Northwind Robotics designs and builds autonomous warehouse carts that move goods between shelves, packing stations and loading docks for third-party logistics providers.

## The role

You will lead a team of four analysts who own fleet performance reporting, customer-facing uptime metrics and the forecasting models that tell our operations team how many carts each site needs next quarter. You will set the team's priorities, review its work, hire as the team grows, and be the person operations leaders call when the numbers disagree. Reports to Taylor Brooks, VP of Operations.

## What we look for

- Four or more years in analytics, including owning a model or metric end to end in production.
- Strong SQL and Python, and experience with a modern warehouse stack (we use dbt and Snowflake).
- Experience mentoring analysts, formally or informally, and explaining results to people who do not read SQL.

## Compensation and visa

Base salary range $150,000 to $175,000. Equity and an annual bonus come on top. Northwind sponsors H-1B transfers and new H-1B petitions for this role. Hybrid, two days a week in the Denver office. The rest of the week is remote.

We read every application. If you are not sure you meet every bullet above, apply anyway and tell us what you would bring.
