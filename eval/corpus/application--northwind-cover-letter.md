# Cover letter: Analytics Lead, Northwind Robotics

Date: 2026-09-27

Dear Taylor Brooks,

Sam Okafor suggested I write to you directly about the Analytics Lead opening (NWR-0912), and I am glad he did: this is the role I have been hoping to find since I moved to Denver.

I have four years of experience in analytics, most recently at Brightline Analytics, where I worked on retention and revenue questions for subscription businesses. Two pieces of that work are the most relevant here. First, my churn model cut monthly churn by 9 percent in the quarter after launch, mostly because customer success used its weekly ranked list to call the right accounts. Second, I led the migration of 140 dbt models to Snowflake, and with it a review process: every model has an owner, tests and a written definition, and changes go through a pull request that someone outside the author's team approves. I care about that kind of process because it is what lets a small team trust its own numbers.

What draws me to Northwind is the chance to build an analytics function rather than inherit one. A fleet of carts generates exactly the kind of data I like working with, and the questions that matter, like uptime, throughput and how many carts a site needs, have direct consequences for customers. I would enjoy leading four analysts toward a shared set of metrics and a habit of checking them.

On logistics: My F-1 OPT STEM extension is valid until 2027-06-30, so I will need H-1B sponsorship, which I understand Northwind offers for this role.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the chance to talk.

Sincerely,
Frank Fu
