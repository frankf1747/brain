# Coffee with Jordan Ellis

Date: 2026-09-24
Where: a coffee shop in LoDo, Denver

Met Jordan Ellis for coffee this morning. He runs the talent program at Beta Ventures and had heard from someone at Northwind that I had moved to Denver, so he reached out. Notes while they are fresh:

- He asked how the Northwind and Acme processes were going. I told him Northwind is my first choice and that the Acme role wants three days in Austin.
- Jordan said Quill Health, another Beta Ventures portfolio company, is hiring a data science manager in Boulder. It is a newer team and more modelling-heavy than the Northwind role. He offered to introduce me to their head of data if I want a third option. I said yes, but not until after the Northwind panel.
- On money: since the Northwind job leads a team of four, he advised me to ask Northwind for a base of at least $160,000 rather than anchoring near my own floor. His view is that lead roles at portfolio companies have been closing in the upper half of their posted range this year.
- He mentioned that Dana Whitfield sits on the boards of both Acme Corp and Quill Health, so the companies talk more than I would have guessed. Worth remembering that whatever I say to one may reach the other.
- He asked me not to share the Quill Health opening until it is posted.
