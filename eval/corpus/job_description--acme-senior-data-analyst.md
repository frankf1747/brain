# Senior Data Analyst, Acme Corp (Austin, TX)

Acme Corp builds industrial drilling equipment, including the ZX-9000 line. We are hiring a Senior Data Analyst to join the Operations Analytics team in Austin.

- Build dashboards for production yield and warranty claims.

- Own the weekly operations review deck.

## Requirements

- 4+ years in analytics; strong SQL and Python.
- Experience with dbt and a modern warehouse (Snowflake or BigQuery).
- Comfortable presenting to executives.

## Compensation and visa

Base salary range $115,000 to $140,000. Acme sponsors H-1B for this role. Hybrid, three days a week in the Austin office. Hiring manager: Priya Natarajan.
