# Acme Corp unveils the ZX-9100 drill

AUSTIN, September 8, 2026. Acme Corp introduced the ZX-9100 on Tuesday at its Austin plant, presenting the rig as the successor to the ZX-9000, the surface drill that made the company's name with quarry and tunnelling contractors. The ZX-9100 is priced at $1.2 million per unit, and Acme says it drills about 30 percent faster in hard rock while using less fuel per metre. The company also claims a simpler maintenance schedule, with the main bearing assembly now replaceable in a single shift.

"Our customers told us the ZX-9000 was reliable but that they wanted more metres per day," CEO Marcus Hale said at the launch event, standing in front of the first production unit. "This is the machine they asked for, and it is built here in Texas."

Deliveries are scheduled to begin in the first quarter of 2027, and Acme said it already holds letters of intent from three mining customers, though it declined to name them. To build the new rig, the expanded Austin facility will add 120 jobs over the next eighteen months, most of them in assembly and field service. The company did not say whether it would continue to sell the ZX-9000 once the new model ships.
