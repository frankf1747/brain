# Acme Corp raises $40M Series B led by Beta Ventures

AUSTIN, March 12, 2026. Acme Corp, maker of the ZX-9000 industrial drill, announced a $40 million Series B round led by Beta Ventures with participation from Lonestar Capital. The company said it will use the funds to expand its Austin manufacturing facility and grow its analytics and software teams by roughly 40 people over the next year.

"Demand for the ZX-9000 has outpaced our capacity for two quarters," said CEO Marcus Hale. Beta Ventures partner Dana Whitfield will join Acme's board.

Acme was founded in 2019 and employs about 300 people.
