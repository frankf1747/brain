# Acme case study panel, transcript

Date: 2026-09-29
Present: Priya Natarajan, Hiring Manager; Luis Ortega, VP of Operations, Acme Corp; Frank Fu (candidate). Video call, 45 minutes.

Priya Natarajan: Thanks for joining, Frank. You had the ZX-9000 warranty extract for a week. Walk us through what you found, and then Luis will have questions.

Frank: Sure. I started with every warranty claim since the ZX-9000 launched and joined it to the build records and the parts list for each unit.

Frank: Failures cluster in units built in the third quarter of 2025, and almost all of those used bearings from Kessler Bearings. The field-failure rate was 4.2 percent for those units against 1.1 percent for the rest.

Luis Ortega: So is it the bearings or the build quarter? We changed a lot on the line that summer.

Frank: I checked that. Units from that quarter with the other supplier's bearings fail at the normal rate, about the same as every other quarter. The units with Kessler bearings from outside that quarter are too few to say much, so I would ask the supplier for their lot records next.

Luis Ortega: That is the answer I was hoping someone would bring us. Different topic: I see from your resume that you are in Denver now?
