# Beta Ventures closes $310 million Fund III

SAN FRANCISCO, September 15, 2026. Beta Ventures has closed its third fund at $310 million, the firm said on Monday, more than double the size of its second fund. Beta invests in industrial and logistics companies, typically leading a Series A or Series B round and taking a board seat. Its portfolio includes Acme Corp and Northwind Robotics. Earlier this year the firm led Acme's $40 million Series B.

"Industrial companies are finally getting the software and data talent they need, and that is where we can help," said partner Dana Whitfield, who sits on several portfolio boards. "Fund III lets us back more of them, and back them for longer."

The firm also plans to expand its talent program, run by Jordan Ellis, which helps portfolio companies hire engineers, analysts and operations leaders. Ellis's team placed more than sixty people across the portfolio last year, according to the firm, and will add two recruiters in Denver and Austin, where several portfolio companies are based. Beta said limited partners in the new fund include university endowments, foundations and two family offices, and that it expects to make twelve to fifteen new investments from Fund III over the next four years.
