# Visa timeline

Date: 2026-09-21

Laying out the calendar so I stop doing this math in my head at 2 a.m.

- My OPT STEM extension ends on 2027-06-30. Until then I can work for any employer enrolled in E-Verify, as long as I keep reporting changes to my school's international office within ten days.
- The next H-1B registration window opens in March 2027. The employer files the registration, not me, so I need an employer who has agreed to sponsor before then.
- If I am selected, the petition is filed from April and, if approved, the H-1B starts on October 1, 2027. Cap-gap should cover the months between June 30 and October 1.
- If I am not selected in the lottery, I have a 60-day grace period after my OPT ends. That is not much time to find another status, so I should think about a backup now, not in June.

Who sponsors: Acme Corp sponsors H-1B for the senior analyst role, and Northwind Robotics sponsors new petitions. The Acme Data Analyst II opening does not sponsor, so it is not an option even as a fallback.

The practical rule: an offer has to land before February so that the new employer's lawyers have time to prepare and the registration can be filed in March. Anything that closes later than that means waiting another full year for the next lottery.

Keep copies of the EAD card, the I-983 training plan and every pay stub in the visa folder.
